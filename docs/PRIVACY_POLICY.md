# PocketLedger Privacy Policy

_Effective 15 November 2026_

This is the same text the app shows under Settings → About.

## Summary

PocketLedger is an offline expense tracker published by Bharavi Vaghjiyani. It does not collect, send, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device, and the publisher cannot see it.

## What the app stores

The transactions, categories and budgets you enter, and your settings (such as language, theme and currency), are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

## No internet access

The app does not request permission to use the internet and has no accounts, advertising, analytics, crash reporting or third-party tracking.

## Notifications

If you turn on budget alerts in Settings, the app asks for permission to show notifications and shows one when a budget reaches the level you chose. Alerts are created on your device from your own data; nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in Settings or in your device's notification settings.

## Backups

On Android, the app's data is excluded from Google cloud backup. On Android 12 and later, a direct transfer from one phone to another while setting up a new phone may copy it.

On iOS, your device's own iCloud or computer backups can include the app's data, as they do for other apps. These backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by the publisher.

## Export and import

When you export transactions, the app creates a CSV file and opens your device's share menu. Where the file goes is your choice, and the app or service you pick has its own privacy terms.

When you import, the app reads only the file you choose.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data. On Android, clearing the app's storage in system settings does the same. Sample data in demo mode is kept separately and deleted when you leave demo mode.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will ship with an app update and show a new effective date.
